Confindential Packaging

We take great care in packaging your order to ensure your items arrive in perfect condition. For international shipments, we use robust carton boxes and padded envelopes to provide the utmost care and privacy during transit. If you prefer discreet packaging, you can easily select this option during the ordering process. More details about our packaging materials are provided below.

Confindentially Packed

Not everyone may wish to disclose the source of their exceptional goods, and we completely respect that. That's why we offer you the choice of discreet packaging. When making your request, simply select the 'discreet packaging' option, and we'll handle the rest. Your items will be carefully packaged in a plain, unbranded carton box or padded envelope, devoid of any logos or our company name. The shipping label will remain as standard, displaying only your address alongside ours, free from any logos or our company name.

Rest assured, we take every measure to safeguard your order during transit. Our preferred choice for packaging is typically the reliable boxes and materials supplied by UPS. This is what you can expect from our standard All Over Piercings packaging.

Envelope

We'll use padded envelopes for smaller parcels. The exterior will be simple, featuring a label with your shipping address and our return address. These envelopes are designed to fit into standard mailbox slots, ensuring convenient delivery without the need for you to be home.

Parcel

Parcels that cannot fit through a standard mailbox slot will be carefully placed inside our exceptional custom boxes. These boxes proudly display our logo and company name on the exterior, complete with an address label containing both your destination and the sender's details. Not only do these boxes ensure the pristine condition of your ordered items, but they also boast an undeniably stylish appearance.
